Parser reads day-first dates as month-first

379 reports across 11 tenants. The shared parser reads every date month-first. For these day-first tenants, that flips the date. The reports came through app (204), email (150), and form (25) channels. The five reports marked as one-day moves in the desk notes also belong to this cause.

Booking-v3 date hint conflicts with tenant locale

33 reports across 3 tenants. These reports came through booking-v3 forms. The form displays MM/DD/YYYY, while the tenants use day-first locales. People follow the hint, then the form interprets the value using the tenant's day-first order and flips it.

Shared parser reads day-first dates as month-first

The shared parser reads dates month-first. For day-first tenants, that reverses the date. These reports arrived through app, email, and form channels. Ticket #1 tracks the parser cause; engineering shipped fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales on 5 October.

Booking-v3 hint conflicts with tenant locale

The booking-v3 form showed MM/DD/YYYY to tenants whose locale is day-first, then interpreted the value using that locale. Ticket #2 tracks this separate cause. The form was retired, so engineering marked it won't fix.
